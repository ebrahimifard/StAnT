🧠 Strategy Knowledge Base & Dictionary

✨ Multi-Factor Ensemble Consensus Institutional Aggregator

Combines signals from all 11 quantitative models using weighted conviction scoring (-100% to +100%). Prevents false signals by requiring cross-strategy consensus across trend, momentum, volatility, and volume features.

Bullish Trigger: Consensus score >= +20% (Strong Buy >= +55%)
Bearish Trigger: Consensus score <= -20% (Strong Sell <= -55%)
⛩️ Ichimoku Kinko Hyo Cloud Breakout Trend & Volatility

Evaluates 5 Japanese components: Tenkan-sen (9-period), Kijun-sen (26-period), Senkou Span A & B (Kumo Cloud), and Chikou Span. Identifies clear macro trend direction, momentum, and dynamic support/resistance zones.

Bullish Trigger: Price breaks above Kumo Cloud & Tenkan crosses above Kijun
Bearish Trigger: Price falls below Kumo Cloud & Tenkan crosses below Kijun
🤖 ML Directional Scoring Model Predictive Quant

Evaluates a multi-feature vector space including Money Flow Index (MFI), RSI slope, MACD velocity, Bollinger %B, and ADX trend strength to compute a predictive bullish probability score (0% to 100%).

Bullish Trigger: Bullish Probability score >= 67.5%
Bearish Trigger: Bullish Probability score <= 32.5%
💎 Keltner Volatility Compression Breakout & Squeeze

Compares Bollinger Bands against ATR-based Keltner Channels to detect low-volatility consolidation squeezes prior to explosive directional breakout moves.

Bullish Trigger: Price breaks above Upper Keltner Channel post-squeeze
Bearish Trigger: Price breaks below Lower Keltner Channel post-squeeze
📍 Parabolic SAR Trailing Reversal Trailing Stop Reversal

Uses an accelerating step factor (0.02 - 0.20) to plot dynamic trailing stop dots that follow price trends and flip positions instantly when trend direction shifts.

Bullish Trigger: Price crosses above SAR dot (dots flip below price)
Bearish Trigger: Price crosses below SAR dot (dots flip above price)
📊 Session VWAP Band Reversion Order Flow Profile

Tracks Volume-Weighted Average Price along with standard deviation volatility bands to identify institutional fair value and overextended mean-reversion opportunities.

Bullish Trigger: Price dips below Lower VWAP Band (-1 Std Dev) & rebounds
Bearish Trigger: Price surges above Upper VWAP Band (+1 Std Dev) & rejects
🚀 Supertrend & ADX Trend Following Trend Following

Combines ATR volatility trailing stop line with ADX trend strength filter (>25) to ride strong trending moves while exiting rapidly on trend exhaustion.

Bullish Trigger: Price crosses above Supertrend line with ADX > 25
Bearish Trigger: Price crosses below Supertrend line
📈 SMA 50/200 Golden & Death Cross Macro Trend

Identifies major long-term institutional bull and bear market regime shifts using 50-day and 200-day Simple Moving Average crossovers.

Golden Cross: 50 SMA crosses above 200 SMA
Death Cross: 50 SMA crosses below 200 SMA
📊 RSI Mean Reversion (14-period) Momentum Oscillator

Measures speed and change of price movements between 0 and 100 to spot extreme oversold conditions for long entries or overbought levels for short exits.

Oversold Buy: RSI < 30 and turning upward
Overbought Sell: RSI > 70 and turning downward

🛡️ Risk Management & Metrics Handbook

ATR Stop-Loss Level

Calculated dynamically using 1.5x Average True Range (ATR). Prevents getting stopped out by random market noise while protecting capital during adverse moves.

Take-Profit Targets (1:2 & 1:3)

Target 1 secures profit at 2x your risk amount. Target 2 captures extended trend moves at 3x risk. Ensures positive expectancy over time.

Win Rate (%) & Profit Factor

Win Rate is the percentage of winning trades. Profit Factor is Gross Profits / Gross Losses. A Profit Factor > 1.5 indicates an institutional-grade edge.

Maximum Drawdown (%)

Measures the largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio equity during the backtest period. Keeps downside exposure within acceptable limits.
